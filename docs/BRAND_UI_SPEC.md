# Chiphi Engineering — Web Brand & UI Specification

## Purpose

The contractor directory is the official web product of the Chiphi Engineering page. It must feel like the same brand people already know from the page, not like a generic contractor directory.

## Brand DNA

### 1. Yellow is the primary brand color
- Yellow is the signature brand color.
- Use it consistently for primary actions, key highlights, badges, and selected brand sections.
- Keep sufficient contrast and restraint so the product remains professional and readable.

### 2. Friendly, cheeky, informal
- The brand is intentionally casual, humorous, and approachable.
- Avoid overly corporate, bureaucratic, or government-style language where a natural Thai phrase works better.
- Small moments of humor are welcome when they do not interfere with a user's ability to find or contact a contractor.

### 3. The "ลุง" personality
- The page owner is commonly called "ลุง" by the audience.
- The relationship with the audience is familiar and conversational.
- Copy may be much more informal than a typical business website, including very casual Thai where appropriate.
- Do not force slang into every sentence. Use it selectively so important business information remains clear.

### 4. Relaxing and entertaining
- The page is a place people visit to have fun and relax.
- The website should preserve some of that feeling while remaining trustworthy when a user is choosing a contractor.
- Humor should be strongest in brand/marketing areas and lighter in transactional areas such as contractor details, reviews, registration, and contact actions.

## Visual Direction

- Primary brand color: yellow.
- Supporting UI should remain clean, neutral, and highly readable.
- Construction/contractor visual cues are appropriate.
- The brand mascot/avatar should remain based on the existing illustrated character: yellow construction helmet, black suit, waving pose, with **44** clearly represented on the helmet.
- Do not replace the mascot with a generic stock construction character.
- Future mascot edits should preserve recognizability and the core character.

## UX Personality by Context

### Homepage / Marketing
More personality is encouraged:
- Friendly headlines.
- Light jokes or conversational microcopy.
- Strong yellow brand moments.
- Clear connection to Chiphi Engineering.

### Search / Contractor discovery
Balanced:
- Friendly language.
- Simple controls.
- Strong visual hierarchy.
- Trust signals must remain obvious.

### Contractor profile / Contact
More professional:
- Keep brand styling and yellow accents.
- Prioritize real contractor information, portfolio, reviews, location, and contact actions.
- Do not let jokes obscure critical information.

### Contractor registration
Professional and reassuring:
- The form may be friendly, but requirements and approval status must be unambiguous.
- Explain that contractor information is reviewed before becoming public.

### Member login
Low friction:
- General members should have a simple sign-in experience, including the planned Facebook Login option.
- Do not make casual visitors fill out a long contractor-style form.

## Copy Principles

- Prefer natural Thai over formal translated/corporate wording.
- Keep sentences short and understandable on mobile.
- Use the brand's conversational voice where appropriate.
- Never sacrifice clarity for a joke.
- Never imply that a contractor is verified/approved unless the database state actually confirms it.

## Product Architecture Principle

The site is a product of the Chiphi Engineering brand. Future phases must reuse this visual and verbal system instead of independently inventing a new style for each page.

Do not redesign the database or authentication architecture merely to implement branding.

## Scope Boundary

This document defines the brand direction. It does not authorize unrelated feature work. Phase requirements, security rules, database constraints, and approval workflows remain authoritative for implementation.

## Future Brand Work

The mascot image may be refined later while preserving the existing character and **44** helmet marking. Any substantial visual redesign should be reviewed before implementation.
